Some of my favorite challenges have been:

<h3>The CRM stopped receiving qualified leads from MCAE</h3>
<H4>Action</H4>
I took ownership and traced the failure across four source systems. I discovered that 99.6% of incoming prospect records missing a single field that blocked qualification entirely. I collaborated with the Dev team to close the data gap, designed a research pipeline with Sales leadership, and got sign off from Business on the new qualification process.
<h4>Result</h4>
The inside sales team received leads again and everyone was happy.

<h3>The new business unit's dedicated IP address needed to be warmed during an active promotion season</h3>
<H4>Action</H4>
I designed an eight-stage IP warming program using engagement-tiered audience expansion with provider-specific tracks, defined growth rates per stage, and tapering cadence, scaling toward 100,000 daily recipients. Executed the program and tracked performance in MCAE, Everest, and Google Postmaster Tools and established a decision checkpoint to fall back to a secondary sending platform — rather than relying on open-ended monitoring.
<h4>Result</h4>
I ran the program under significant constraints — no subject matter expert available, concurrent live campaign load, and creative with no prior send history — and documented the negative authentication and reputation issues that resulted. The effort was not successful but I include it as a favorite challenge because I learned so much from the project and am confident my next IP Warming efforts will end with success.
